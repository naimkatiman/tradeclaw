# TradeClaw brand specification

TradeClaw is an open trading research lab. Its public value is the cost-adjusted evidence trail: what was tested, what failed, and how another person can reproduce it.

## Positioning

- Primary promise: Test trading ideas. See where they fail after costs. Verify every result.
- Category: Open-source trading research platform.
- Never present the hosted site as a live signal feed, broker, or paid trading service.
- Lead with observed evidence and explicit limitations before tools or calls to action.

## Information architecture

- Evidence: Track Record, Studies, Prospective Ledger, Methodology, Open Data.
- Lab: Candidate Feed, Screener, Backtest.
- Build: Self-host, Documentation, API, GitHub.

## Visual system

`DESIGN.md` is the canonical visual system. Preserve the existing TradeClaw logo, emerald accent, Geist typography, dense evidence surfaces, and dark/light tokens. Use amber only for methodological caveats and rose only for negative evidence. Motion is restrained and must honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Product language

- Use “research candidate” instead of “signal” when a row has not passed deployment gates.
- Use “reference levels” instead of “entry,” “stop loss,” or “take profit” on public research surfaces.
- Use “prospective ledger” instead of language that implies live execution.
- Prefer “modeled costs,” “observed OHLCV outcome,” and “not deployable” when those are the facts.

## Assets

- Logo components and marks in `apps/web/components/` are canonical.
- Editorial evidence imagery in `apps/web/public/brand/editorial/` is approved for research storytelling.
- Do not introduce stock trading imagery, profit screenshots, badges, streaks, or star-progress visuals.
