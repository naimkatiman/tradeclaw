## Proposal 3 — Turn the study's QA gates into a standing, publishable trust signal (new this run)

The study nearly shipped a wrong verdict from stale June candles — exactly the class of error the external literature says produces "institutional-grade" curves that "collapse immediately in live conditions." It was caught only because a pre-registered reconciliation gate, a lookahead assertion, and a 7-day staleness guard were in place. Codify those three checks as standing, machine-run gates in the research pipeline (`scripts/research`, read-only on product), and publish their pass/fail status alongside any published study (starting with P1's entry): "reconciliation gate: PASS (n, gross, cost, net vs dashboard) · lookahead: PASS · staleness: 0 unclassified-stale." This converts internal QA into a visible, falsifiable trust marker — the OSS analog of a third-party "verified" stamp, but showing the check itself, not just the result.

- Brand-alignment: "Every trade verified" and "measurable proof" applied to the research process itself — the reader sees that the study's own integrity checks passed, not just its conclusion.
- Evidence: repo — study RESULTS §Data-quality incident (the byte-identical-table near-miss, the staleness guard, the reconciliation gate that already gates interpretation), `scripts/research/regime-study-assembly.ts` + its test (already in-tree, dirty). External — look-ahead/stale-data leakage is the dominant cause of backtests that fail live, and reproducible pipelines enforce strict point-in-time information sets ([Freqtrade lookahead analysis](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/lookahead-analysis/); [Backtesting mistakes that kill quant strategies](https://hedgefundalpha.com/education/backtesting-mistakes-kill-quant-strategies-guide/)).
- Effort/Impact: medium effort (promote three existing ad-hoc checks into a reusable gate module + a one-line status surface per published study). High impact — makes the difference between a study that looks rigorous and one that is provably rigorous, and prevents a future silent stale-data verdict.
- Grill verdict: SURVIVED — attacked as "internal dev tooling, not user-facing, out of scope for a transparency product." The near-miss is precisely the failure mode that discredits trading claims; publishing the gate status is the user-facing payoff. Fidelity caveat: keep the public surface to a per-study status line, not a new subsystem, or it becomes scope creep.

## Proposal 4 — Position the pre-registered kill-ledger as a claimed transparency standard (new this run; market/competitive)

TradeClaw already owns something the retail-signal market structurally does not: a pre-registered, results-agnostic, machine-readable kill-ledger — a registered-report analog. Competitors "manipulate their historical results" and, at best, prove wins via a third-party monitor; none pre-commits a hypothesis and gate and then publishes the verdict regardless. With P1 (publish the newest kill) and P3 (publish the gate status) in place, make the practice an explicit, claimed position: "We write the hypothesis and the pass/fail gate down and timestamp them before we run, then publish the verdict whether it passes or fails" — provable by the committed, timestamped specs. This is positioning grounded in an existing falsifiable mechanism, not a new feature.

- Brand-alignment: it is the brand's core claim stated as a defensible market standard — "measurable proof over marketing spin" becomes a named practice competitors can't cheaply copy because it requires publishing your failures.
- Evidence: repo — the five committed pre-registered specs + verdict memos already linked from `/research`, plus the 07-17/07-18/08-05 plan docs as timestamped pre-registrations. External — registered reports reduce publication bias by making publication results-agnostic ([Royal Society Registered Reports](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsos/pages/registered-reports); [Benefits of preregistration and Registered Reports, 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2833373X.2024.2376046)); the retail norm is manipulated histories with third-party verification as the only honesty bar ([Best Forex Signal Providers 2026, ForexBrokers.com](https://www.forexbrokers.com/guides/forex-signals-providers)); 90%+ of academic strategies fail live, so a public negative-results bench is a credibility moat ([Interpretable Hypothesis-Driven Trading, arXiv 2512.12924](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12924v1)).
- Effort/Impact: low-medium effort (mostly copy + a small "how we register research" explainer; the mechanism already exists). High impact — converts an existing engineering practice into a stated, hard-to-copy market differentiator.
- Grill verdict: SURVIVED — attacked as "positioning is marketing spin, off-brand for a proof-first project." The claim is backed by committed, timestamped, results-agnostic specs and (with P3) a machine-checked gate — falsifiable, not spin. Fidelity caveat: the claim must stay "we pre-register and publish regardless of outcome," never "we have a winning strategy" — the ledger currently shows only kills, and that is the point.
