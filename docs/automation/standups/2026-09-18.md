## HEADLINE — the EMA200 defect is confirmed on the live endpoint at n=4, and 3 of the 4 signals carry it

This morning's weekly (`028f020d`) reported that the published `ema200` is silently the entry price whenever the candle window is under 200 bars, and confirmed it against the **single** signal the endpoint was then serving, with the n=1 caveat stated in its own body. Today the endpoint serves **four**. The finding survives the larger sample and gets stronger.

Raw values from `GET https://tradeclaw.win/api/signals` at `2026-09-18T01:32:33.306Z`, `count: 4`, `engine.real: 4`, `engine.fallback: 0`:

| Symbol | Conf | entry | ema200 | ema20 | ema50 | `ema200 === entry`? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NVDAUSD | 78 | `219.39999` | `219.39999` | `218.94146` | `217.49541` | **yes** |
| AMDUSD | 75 | `544.78003` | `544.78003` | `543.23251` | `533.90783` | **yes** |
| AAPLUSD | 51 | `337.07999` | `337.07999` | `335.72752` | `334.34452` | **yes** |
| BNBUSD | 75 | `741.7` | `724.86656` | `736.42517` | `732.78716` | no |

**Three of four published signals ship a fabricated EMA200.** In all three the `ema20` and `ema50` are ordinary, distinct, plausible values — only the 200-period figure collapses onto the entry. That signature is what the source trace predicts, and I re-verified all four links in the primary tree rather than inheriting them from the weekly:

1. `apps/web/app/lib/ta-engine.ts:78-80` — `calcEMA` allocates `new Array(data.length).fill(NaN)` and **returns that array unchanged** when `data.length < period`.
2. `apps/web/app/lib/ta-engine.ts:259,268` — `last200 = ema200.filter(v => !isNaN(v))` is therefore empty, so `current.ema200` resolves to the `: NaN` arm.
3. `apps/web/app/lib/signal-generator.ts:605` — `ema200: +(emaCurrent.ema200 || currentPrice).toFixed(5)`. **`NaN` is falsy**, so `NaN || currentPrice` yields `currentPrice`. The substitution is a `||` coercion, not an explicit fallback.
4. `apps/web/app/lib/signal-generator.ts:920,931` — `const entry = primary.indicators.closes[closes.length - 1]`, passed into `buildIndicatorSummary(primary.indicators, entry)` as `currentPrice`, and emitted as `entry: +entry.toFixed(5)`.

Both fields are `.toFixed(5)` of the same variable, so `ema200 === entry` **by construction**, not by coincidence. The byte-identical match in three live signals is the predicted observable.

**The generator's own floor is the mechanism.** `signal-generator.ts:660` reads `if (indicators.closes.length < 100) return []`. The minimum candle count is **100** — exactly half the period of the longest indicator the same function publishes. Every signal generated in the 100–199 bar band publishes a fabricated EMA200. Lines 603 and 604 carry the identical `||` pattern for `ema20`/`ema50`; those never misfire only because their periods (20, 50) sit below the 100-bar floor. The bug is general; the floor hides two thirds of it.

**BNBUSD is the control case, and it strengthens the trace.** It is the one symbol with a genuine EMA200, and it is the one symbol that is 24/7 crypto. The other three are equity-style symbols, and their signal timestamps are all `2026-09-17T15:45:00.000Z` — the US session close — while BNBUSD's is `2026-09-18T01:15:00.000Z`, current. Equity symbols accumulate M15 bars only during market hours and so sit in the 100–199 band; a 24/7 symbol clears 200 easily. The causal link is labelled **inferred** — I did not read the candle counts directly — but it is a 4-for-4 match on the observable and it is exactly what the mechanism predicts.

**Scope, stated honestly.** This is a **disclosure** defect, not a trading defect. The scorer guards `!isNaN` on the raw value and correctly abstains, so the fabricated number does not drive direction or sizing. What it does do is ship to `/dashboard`, the explain surface, the public API docs, and any agent webhook that type-checks it as a valid number. Under "Every trade verified", publishing the entry price relabelled as a 200-period moving average is precisely the failure mode the brand exists to refuse.
