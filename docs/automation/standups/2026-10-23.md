## Headline: the "published record shrank" anomaly is the 10,000-row cap working as coded, not an integrity event

Today's weekly (`weekly-research-2026-10-23.md:153`) recorded `totalSignals` falling 7,797 → 7,785 in 7 h 18 m and called it a defect: "A cumulative record cannot shrink." That framing is wrong. The number is not cumulative.

Measured this run, two reads 32 minutes apart:

| # | `updatedAt` | `totalSignals` | Correct (derived) | `windowStart` | `windowEnd` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (stale) | 2026-10-23T01:01:15.311Z | 7,785 | 2,803 | 07-06T14:00Z | 10-22T21:45Z |
| 2 (fresh) | 2026-10-23T01:33:22.723Z | 7,782 | 2,803 | 07-06T14:15Z | 10-22T21:45Z |

Integrity: `7785 × 0.36005138086062943 = 2803.000` and `7782 × 0.36019018247237217 = 2803.000`, exact integers. Three rows left the population. All three were losses, because correct stayed at 2,803. `windowEnd` did not move, so no newly resolved row entered. `windowStart` moved forward 15 minutes, one signal batch.

Mechanism, read from `origin/main`:

- `apps/web/lib/signal-history.ts:400-406`: `readHistoryAsync()` selects `WHERE is_simulated = FALSE AND <not dark> ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 10_000`. There is no resolved filter in SQL.
- `apps/web/app/api/calibration/route.ts:50-53`: the route filters `isCountedResolved` and confidence ≥ 0.5 *after* the cap. `windowStart`/`windowEnd` are min/max over that filtered set (`:83-85`).

So each new pending or gate-blocked row takes one of the 10,000 slots and pushes out the oldest row. If the evicted row was counted-resolved, the published total drops. Read 2 shows exactly that pattern. [DERIVED] 10,000 − 7,782 = 2,218 slots (22%) are held by rows the route does not count: pending, gate-blocked, zero force-expiry placeholders, or confidence below 0.5. The split is not observed.

What this changes: the carried 10-16 P1 (disclose the 10,000-row window) is still the defect. The weekly's "cannot shrink" sub-claim is retracted. The owner fix also narrows. Either count the cap in resolved rows (move the resolved predicate into SQL before `LIMIT`), or label the figure "resolved signals among the newest 10,000 rows". Product code, owner call. This run does not touch it.
