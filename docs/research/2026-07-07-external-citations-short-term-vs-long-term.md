# External citation pack: short-term trading vs long-term holding

Date: 2026-07-07
Purpose: sourced evidence base for the transparency pivot (`docs/plans/2026-07-07-free-open-source-transparency-pivot.md`), feeding the future `/why-long-term` page. Primary or near-primary sources only, located and cross-checked 2026-07-07. Do not add claims without a URL.

## Active management vs index

- **SPIVA U.S. Scorecard (S&P Dow Jones Indices, YE2024/YE2025)** — 89.5% of actively managed US large-cap funds underperformed the S&P 500 over the 15 years ending 2024-12-31; zero of 22 US equity categories had a majority of active managers beat their benchmark over 15 years; 79% underperformed in calendar 2025 alone. Underperformance rises with horizon.
  https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/spiva/article/spiva-us/

## Individual-trader outcomes

- **Barber & Odean, "Trading Is Hazardous to Your Wealth", Journal of Finance 55(2), 2000** — 66,465 households, 1991–96: average household 16.4%/yr vs market 17.9%; most-active quintile 11.4%/yr (−6.5pp/yr). "Those who trade the most are hurt the most."
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-1082.00226
  Paywall-free author copy: https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/returns/individual_investor_performance_final.pdf
- **Barber, Lee, Liu & Odean, "Just How Much Do Individual Investors Lose by Trading?", RFS 22(2), 2009 (Taiwan, complete market)** — individual trading losses = 2.2% of Taiwan GDP; institutions gain +1.5pp/yr from the other side of those trades.
  https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/22/2/609/1595677
- **Barber, Lee, Liu & Odean, "The Cross-Section of Speculator Skill" (Taiwan day traders)** — fewer than 1% of the day-trader population predictably earns positive abnormal returns net of fees in a given year.
  https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/The%20Cross-Section%20of%20Speculator%20Skill.pdf
- **Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti, "Day Trading for a Living?" (Brazil, SSRN 3423101)** — of individuals persisting 300+ days in equity-futures day trading, 97% lost money; 1.1% earned more than minimum wage; 0.5% more than a bank teller. No evidence of learning by day trading.
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101

## Behavior gap

- **DALBAR QAIB 2025** — average equity fund investor earned 16.54% in 2024 vs S&P 500 25.02% (848bp gap, second-largest in a decade). Dollar-weighted investor returns, not fund returns.
  https://www.dalbar.com/press-release/investors-missed-the-best-of-2024s-market-gains-latest-dalbar-investor-behavior-report-finds/
- **DALBAR QAIB 2026 (calendar 2025)** — ten-year view: ~9.8%/yr investor vs ~13%/yr S&P 500; 2025 one-year gap narrowed to 72bp (17.16% vs 17.88%).
  https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dalbars-2026-qaib-report-shows-narrower-investor-gap-amid-a-complex-and-volatile-market-year-302745998.html
  CAVEAT: DALBAR's methodology has published critics — pair with SPIVA, never lead with it.

## Product-level loss rates

- **ESMA intervention, March 2018** — EU national regulators' analyses cited by ESMA: 74–89% of retail CFD accounts lose money, average losses €1,600–29,000/client. Origin of the mandated "XX% of retail investor accounts lose money" warning. ATTRIBUTION NOTE: cite as "EU regulators' analyses cited by ESMA", not "an ESMA study".
  https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-agrees-prohibit-binary-options-and-restrict-cfds-protect-retail-investors
- **UK FCA CP16/40, December 2016** — 82% of sampled retail CFD clients lose money; average loss £2,200.
  https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/consultation/cp16-40.pdf
- **Bryzgalova, Pavlova & Sikorskaya, "Retail Trading in Options and the Rise of the Big Three Wholesalers", Journal of Finance 2023** — retail options traders lost ~$2.1B Nov 2019–Jun 2021; aggregate trading costs $6.4B; retail-preferred weeklies carry 12.6% average bid-ask spread; three wholesalers handle ~85% of options PFOF.
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jofi.13285
- **BIS Working Paper 1049 (95 countries, 2015–2022)** — estimated 73–81% of retail crypto-app users lost money on bitcoin; new users disproportionately young/male, entered after price rises; on-chain data shows whales selling into retail buying.
  https://www.bis.org/publ/work1049.htm

## Structural asymmetry ("rules written for bigger money") — defensible framing only

- **SEC v. Robinhood, press release 2020-321** — PFOF-driven inferior execution deprived customers of $34.1M net of commission savings; $65M penalty. The documented case that "commission-free" has a hidden execution cost.
  https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2020-321
- **SEC DERA working paper, "How Does Payment for Order Flow Influence Markets?" (2025)** — SEC's own economists on the PFOF/best-execution tension. Context (CRS IF12594): PFOF is banned in the UK, Australia, and Canada; EU agreed a phase-in ban.
  https://www.sec.gov/files/dera_wp_payment-order-flow-2501.pdf
- **FINRA pattern day trader rule, 2001 → 2026-06-04 (Regulatory Notice 26-10)** — $25k minimum equity for 4+ day trades per 5 business days bound only small accounts for 25 years. ELIMINATED effective 2026-06-04 (replaced by exposure-proportional intraday margin). COPY RULE: past tense only.
  https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10
