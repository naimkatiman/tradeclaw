**Inflow = 59 rows** (offsets 0–58). 10-24 was a Saturday. Every probed row inside the window is crypto; the first equity row appears at offset 80, on Friday's US session. The feed had gaps: no row between 01:45Z and 05:15Z, and the newest row was 106 min old at read time.

[DERIVED] With the cap full on both days, 59 rows in means 59 evicted. Those 59 rows spanned 13.0 h of the July tail, which is 108.9 rows/day. That sits below the tail's 122–127/day average and far above the ~14/day 07-06 → 07-13 week. The thin week is gone from the cap and the window is now evicting the dense stretch.

[DERIVED] Differencing each period count against the measured inflow gives the row count of the day that aged out of each period:

|---|---|---|---|

The two weekend days are low and the weekday is high. That fits a weekend inflow dip (crypto-only rows while FX, metals and equities are shut). It rests on three derived points, not a measured weekday profile.

Consequence: the published window **lengthened for the first time in this series**, 102.52 → 103.00 days. `windowEnd` moved 24.5 h and `windowStart` 13.0 h. The window's span depends on daily inflow against the density of the tail being evicted, so day-to-day snapshots remain non-comparable.

**Falsifiable prediction for the 10-26 daily.** The inflow window 10-25T01:30Z → 10-26T01:30Z is a UTC Sunday: equities stay shut and FX reopens only around 21–22Z.
