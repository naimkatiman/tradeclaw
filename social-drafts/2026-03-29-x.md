## Post 2

A score like 80/100 is an engine rule score. It is not an 80% chance of profit.

Candidate generation, alert delivery, paper ledgers, broker orders, and broker fills are separate states.

## Post 3

Historical stats use counted 24h OHLCV-resolved outcomes. Simulated, blocked, unresolved, and force-expired placeholder rows are excluded.

That is still not broker execution or portfolio performance.
