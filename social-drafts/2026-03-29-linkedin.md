# LinkedIn Draft - TradeClaw

I am building TradeClaw, an MIT-licensed platform for generating and auditing rule-scored trading candidates.

The part I care about most is provenance. Each public result should answer:

- Which configured data source produced the inputs?
- Which rules contributed to the candidate score?
- Which later OHLCV observation resolved the outcome?
- Which rows were excluded from the denominator?

TradeClaw's counted statistics are 24-hour OHLCV-resolved signal outcomes. They are not broker fills or portfolio returns, and a rule score is not a probability of profit.

The source can be self-hosted with Docker Compose after reviewing the prerequisites. MIT covers the code; hosting, data, messaging, model, and broker providers can have separate costs and terms.
