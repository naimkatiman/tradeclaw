# LinkedIn Draft - Outcome Methodology Update

TradeClaw now treats public performance copy as a data contract, not a marketing number.

The counted population includes eligible persisted candidates with a real 24-hour OHLCV-resolved outcome. It excludes simulated, gate-blocked, unresolved, and force-expired zero-PnL placeholders.

I am deliberately not pasting a static win rate into this post. A rate becomes stale, and without its sample, window, source, and exclusions it is easy to misread.

The current methodology and sourced result surfaces are here:

https://tradeclaw.win/methodology
https://tradeclaw.win/track-record

Important boundary: these are signal-outcome research measurements. They are not broker fills, do not reproduce every trading cost, and do not establish portfolio profitability.
